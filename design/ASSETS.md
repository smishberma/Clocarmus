# Main player assets

The sample album cover and individual player glyphs in `app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/` were extracted from the supplied `01-main-player.png`. Glyph backgrounds are transparent; the screen layout, text, clock, sliders, touch targets, and control state are rendered by Compose. Album art and metadata are demo fixtures until the local music library is implemented.

Inter 4.1 regular and bold are bundled as static TTF fonts for Android 6 compatibility. Source: https://github.com/rsms/inter/releases/tag/v4.1. Inter is distributed under the SIL Open Font License; see [the included license](licenses/Inter-LICENSE.txt).
